# COMPLETE FRONTEND DESIGN SPECIFICATION
# Smugflex AI Academy — AI Powered Interactive Learning Platform

## ROLE

Act as a Principal Product Designer, UX Architect, SaaS Design System Engineer, and EdTech Product Specialist.

Design a complete production-ready frontend for:

**Smugflex AI Academy**

A next-generation AI-powered education platform where students purchase courses, learn with AI tutors, practice skills interactively, complete assignments, build projects, receive AI evaluation, and earn verified certificates.

The design must be unique, premium, modern, scalable, and ready for React + TypeScript implementation.

Do not copy Udemy, Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, YouTube, or existing platforms.
